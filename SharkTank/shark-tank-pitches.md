## 🦈 WU WinBack — Predict. Recover. Retain.

Hello Sharks! 🦈

Recently, a friend told me he stopped using Western Union because **Apple Pay wasn't working when he tried to send money.** Instead of waiting, he simply switched to a competitor.

That got me thinking.

**How many customers are we losing without even realizing it?**

Sometimes it's a payment failure, sometimes it's fees or exchange rates, and sometimes it's simply a better experience elsewhere.

Introducing **WU WinBack — an AI-powered customer retention and recovery engine.**

By leveraging our existing transaction data, failed payment attempts, and customer behavior, we could identify customers at risk of leaving and proactively bring them back.

For example, if Apple Pay fails during a transaction, why not notify the customer once it's restored and make it easier to complete their transfer?

**Not every customer needs a discount. Sometimes, fixing the experience is enough.**

From a business perspective, this means fewer lost customers, more completed transactions, stronger retention, and increased customer lifetime value.

We could start with a 90-day pilot and measure recovered transactions, retention, and incremental revenue.

Sharks, **we invest heavily in acquiring customers. But are we doing enough to keep the ones we already have?**

**Let's stop just tracking transactions and start protecting customer relationships.**

Thank you! 🦈
